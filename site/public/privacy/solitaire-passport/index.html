Solitaire Passport: TriPeaks — Privacy Policy

ZetSoft LLC · Effective October 7, 2026

Solitaire Passport: TriPeaks (“the app”) is a solitaire game for iPhone and for Android phones and tablets (Google Play package com.zetsoft.solitairepassport), made by ZetSoft LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: we have no accounts, no servers that store your data, and no analytics. The app is free and supported by ads from Google and optional in-app purchases through Apple (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android).

1. Information we do not collect

We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. The app has no sign-in. We don't run analytics or crash-reporting services of our own, and we don't sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device and in your own cloud backup

Your game progress — levels, gems, boosters, stamps, card backs, purchase records, settings and statistics — is saved on your device. We cannot see or access this data.

iPhone: If iCloud is turned on, a copy is also saved in your own iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage) so progress carries over to a new device or a reinstall. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Android: There is no iCloud on Android. If Android backup is turned on, the game's progress file is included in your own Google account backup and in device-to-device transfers, so progress can carry over to a new device. Google's handling of backup data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK on both iPhone and Android. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier — on iPhone, the IDFA, only if you allow tracking in Apple's “Allow Tracking” prompt; on Android, the Android advertising ID (Android has no “Allow Tracking” prompt; see below for how to reset or delete it);
  • on Android, where available on your device, information from Android's Privacy Sandbox ad features, such as Topics (broad interest categories estimated on your device) and attribution reporting (measuring whether an ad led to an action);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) can be estimated;
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them, and basic device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • diagnostic and performance information about the SDK.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads are requested, on both iPhone and Android, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices in the app. If you deny tracking or consent, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. On iPhone, you can turn off tracking for all apps in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can also manage Privacy Sandbox ad features if your device has them (menu names can vary slightly by device).

Buying Remove Ads turns off interstitial and banner ads. Optional rewarded videos (watched for gems or an extra chance) remain available.

4. In-app purchases (Apple and Google Play)

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (on iPhone) or by Google Play Billing (on Android). We never receive your payment details.

iPhone: The app stores the transaction identifiers Apple provides so each purchase is credited exactly once and Remove Ads can be restored.

Android: The app stores the Google Play purchase tokens so each purchase is credited exactly once and Remove Ads and the Starter Pack can be restored.

5. Game settings, updates and ratings

When the app starts, it downloads a small settings file from our website (zetsoft.dev). On iPhone, it also checks the App Store for a newer version. On Android, the update check uses only that settings file — the app makes no request to Google Play for it. These requests contain no personal information or identifiers. As with any web request, our hosting provider (and, on iPhone, Apple) see your IP address and keep it briefly in standard server logs.

On Android, the app may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review dialog. The dialog is run by Google Play: your rating and any review go to Google Play under Google's terms, and the app receives nothing from it.

7. Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Tracking (iPhone): answer “Ask App Not to Track”, or change it later in iOS Settings.
  • Advertising ID (Android): reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Ad consent (EEA/UK/CH): app Settings → Privacy choices, on both iPhone and Android.
  • Delete your data (iPhone): delete the app to remove local data. To remove the iCloud copy, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage (or “Manage Account Storage”) and delete the app's data.
  • Delete your data (Android): uninstall the app to remove local data. The copy in your Google account backup is managed in your phone's Google backup settings (Google One), where you can turn backup off or delete the backup.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we hold no personal data on our side, requests about ad data are best directed to Google; we're happy to help with any question.
