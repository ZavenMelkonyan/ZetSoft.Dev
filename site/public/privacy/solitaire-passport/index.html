Solitaire Passport: TriPeaks — Privacy Policy

ZetSoft LLC · Effective October 1, 2026

Solitaire Passport: TriPeaks (“the app”) is a solitaire game for iPhone made by ZetSoft LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: we have no accounts, no servers that store your data, and no analytics. The app is free and supported by ads from Google and optional in-app purchases through Apple.

1. Information we do not collect

We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. The app has no sign-in. We don't run analytics or crash-reporting services of our own, and we don't sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device and in your iCloud

Your game progress — levels, gems, boosters, stamps, card backs, purchase records, settings and statistics — is saved on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on, a copy is also saved in your own iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage) so progress carries over to a new device or a reinstall. We cannot see or access this data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in Apple's “Allow Tracking” prompt;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) can be estimated;
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them, and basic device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • diagnostic and performance information about the SDK.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices in the app. If you deny tracking or consent, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can turn off tracking for all apps in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Buying Remove Ads turns off interstitial and banner ads. Optional rewarded videos (watched for gems or an extra chance) remain available.

4. In-app purchases (Apple)

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The app stores the transaction identifiers Apple provides so each purchase is credited exactly once and Remove Ads can be restored.

5. Game settings and updates

When the app starts, it downloads a small settings file from our website (zetsoft.dev) and checks the App Store for a newer version. These requests contain no personal information or identifiers. As with any web request, our hosting provider and Apple see your IP address and keep it briefly in standard server logs.

7. Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Tracking: answer “Ask App Not to Track”, or change it later in iOS Settings.
  • Ad consent (EEA/UK/CH): app Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Delete your data: delete the app to remove local data. To remove the iCloud copy, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage (or “Manage Account Storage”) and delete the app's data.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we hold no personal data on our side, requests about ad data are best directed to Google; we're happy to help with any question.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
